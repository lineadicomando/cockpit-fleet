# The interface: history

Archive: what was tried, measured and found while building the interface;
not needed to work on it.

**25 October**, on the Debian VM, in Italian, with data from `checks.sh`
and a project, a template and a schedule: Lighthouse scored accessibility
100 on every tab, on the page of a host with a chart open, on a run, on
the modal of a new template, and in the dark theme; a scan of the DOM on
every tab and on each modal for a new item found no English text of
PatternFly, no label pointing at nothing and no field without a name.
